Carry the places you love.

Porcelain tumblers carved by hand with the outlines of Northern California's landmarks: the ridge of Mt. Tamalpais, the redwoods of Muir Woods, the shoreline of Lake Tahoe and the Golden Gate.

The Landmark Collection

Each tumbler is thrown on the wheel, then carved by hand with a single line that traces a place, so no two are quite the same. Sized for coffee, wine or a handful of wildflowers.

On location

Made to be taken home to the place itself.

Every design starts from a real view: the Tam ridge from the Marin marshes at golden hour, the Golden Gate from the beach, the redwoods of Muir Woods.

Also from the studio

Alongside the tumblers: small batches of vases, bowls and one-off pieces. Glazes in ocean blue, cobalt brushwork, crackle and speckled clay. Available pieces are listed on Etsy.

About

Hi, I'm Karo.

I'm a ceramicist based in the Bay Area. Everything here is thrown, trimmed and carved by hand in my small home studio.

The landmark tumblers began with Mt. Tam, the mountain I see every day. I wanted a cup that held a little of that view. Since then the collection has grown to the places people here love most.

Have a place that means something to you? I'm always open to custom designs.
